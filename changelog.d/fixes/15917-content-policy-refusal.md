- **fix(resilience):** Keep structured content-policy refusals (`cyber_policy` / `content_policy_violation`) request-scoped: they now surface as a 400 with their public error code instead of a synthetic 502, and no longer cool the account, trip the provider breaker or trigger a same-target stream retry. Stopping the whole combo on such a refusal (instead of advancing to the next target) is opt-in via the new `CONTENT_POLICY_REFUSAL_COMBO_STOP_ENABLED` flag, default off ([#15917](https://github.com/diegosouzapw/OmniRoute/pull/15917)) — thanks @insoln.
