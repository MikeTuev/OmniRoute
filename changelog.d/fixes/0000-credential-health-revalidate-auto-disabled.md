- **fix(credential-health):** opt-in retest of connections the gateway switched off by itself (OAuth refresh retry budget, stream expiry, missing Antigravity project, ...). With the `CREDENTIAL_HEALTH_RETEST_AUTO_DISABLED` feature flag on (default off), such a switch-off is marked `autoDisabledAt` and the credential-health sweep retests it at most once per 6 h each and 5 per sweep; a passing test switches it back on. Operator-disabled connections, accounts switched off by the permanent-ban auto-disable (`banDisabledAt`), banned or deactivated accounts and inactive rows with no marker are never retested ([#16190](https://github.com/diegosouzapw/OmniRoute/issues/16190))
